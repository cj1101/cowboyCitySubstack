## 2. Engine reality vs. the design — the delivery problem

The landed ripening machinery cannot deliver a survey as the design prescribes, and this module does not pretend otherwise:

1. `PENDING_KINDS` (`pendingRules.ts`) is a closed record (innovation-breakthrough / delayed-stat-effect / instant-effect). The design's three delivery kinds (C-8) are Lead registry additions — specced exactly in §5.
2. `validatePayloadSpec` accepts only determinate (stat-delta) or generative (Rubar-bounds) specs, and `applyRipeningEffect` applies only stat deltas / fund disbursements / a city-field credit. A delivery's effect — writing an intel record, evaluating discovery, paying an insider — is none of those.
3. `resolveRipening` hardcodes `descriptorId: "R-33"` and `covertEntriesAllowed: false`. A discovered delivery's plan is MIXED (actor-private entries + one public discovery entry) and needs its visibility flag from the frozen spec.

**The composition (the chains E2 precedent — reference behavior in the module, engine wiring at assembly):** commission handlers build delivery `PendingDraft`s directly in the landed doc shape — mirroring `buildPendingDraft` field-for-field — because that builder's audience default would seat the TARGET's owners in a covert pending's `audienceUids`, and pending reads are audience-fenced in the landed rules: the default would leak a covert run to its target (flag F-A5). The module exports `buildDeliveryPlan(snapshot, pendingDoc, { prepared, citableBaseline })` as the full delivery plan builder; the harness proves it end-to-end through the real `validatePlan`; §5 items 2–3 spec the Lead's ripening wiring precisely (kind registration, snapshot assembly incl. `spec.readPaths` + the counter-means query, kind-keyed dispatch, the spec-driven covert flag).

A second landed constraint shaped the read plans: materialization is ONE pass (`collectActionReadPlan` over base seeds). Read plans therefore steer from the payload (`personCityId`, `claimPublicationId`, `publisherCityId`, `publicationPath` per the landed seed convention) and every handler cross-validates its steers against the documents actually read — a lying steer can only produce a rejection, never a wrong write (fail-closed; gate battery covers the mismatch paths by construction of the checks).

## 3. Interpretation flags (surfaced, not silently flipped)

- **F-A1 — Vehicle composition.** `establish_means` cites EXISTING vehicle records only; it does not wrap other families' handlers (no family exports a plan-contribution helper, and cross-handler wrapping has no landed precedent). Activation is derived; the stored status flip lands only inside later acquisition events (dissolution/discovery).
- **F-A2 — buildMeans voiding.** The buildMeans delivery pending carries three landed precondition clauses: means-record-exists → void, means status != dissolved → void, log-counter `lastSeq > activatesAtSeq − 1` → wait. Voiding refunds `debit − buildCost` via the spec's `voidEffects` (the build money is committed; the operating portion is not). Existing-means commissions carry NO status precondition (design §3.4) — a meanwhile-dissolved domestic means still delivers, its terminal status noted in `reliability.notes`.
- **F-A3 — Where lie consequences land in Phase 4.** Design §11.3/C-2 puts consequence *application* on the Phase 7 track, but gate P4-G8-C8 requires the consequence to land in Phase 4 as a Resolution Event citing the report. Reconciled via the member adoption action (v2's own uptake forms are "verdict R-23 or Town Hall adoption" — adoption is that act in Phase 4 form; the tribunal track refines it in Phase 7). **F-A3b:** adoption re-derives verdicts from the claim + the report's DECLARED found figures/evidence bands (the adopter cannot lawfully read the verifier's private intel — the evidence fence binds R-31, where the verifier must hold it). A fabricated report is itself a publication, falsifiable by the same machinery. Discovery's stat costs (law −2 / influence −1) are NOT applied in Phase 4 (design §7.4 explicit): the discovery entry records them as `consequencesDue … appliedBy: "phase-7-consequence-track"`, and the harness asserts the target's stats are byte-unchanged.
- **F-A4 — Detection thresholds [the §11.2 mechanics].** Falsity is established only when evidence strength suffices for the divergence: band D never; band C only divergence > 10%; band B/A any numeric divergence (non-numeric: B/A only). This makes TC-P13(ii) mechanical — a wholesale lie is provable at band C, a ≤10% spin is not — and the harness proves both directions plus D-proves-nothing.
- **F-A5 — Manual pending drafts.** See §2. Covert audience = actor + actor-city owners ONLY; public domestic deliveries seat origin + target owners.
- **F-A6 — Situation intents.** `validatePlan` rejects `narrativeOutputs` kinds other than `"note"` in resolver-built plans, so the design's `"situation"` kind cannot be emitted from handlers today. Suspected/discovered intents ride kind `"note"` with a `situation-intent:` text prefix; the Phase 5 consumer keys on the prefix + the manifest. Flagged for the Phase 5 build lead.
- **F-A7 — Publication doc ownership.** The stamp's `publishedAtSeq` is carried in the stamp entry's classification (= the stamp resolution's summary seq), not written onto the member's doc — the design C-5 rules amendment (fabric may stamp `publishedAtSeq`) remains a Lead rules item if wanted; nothing here needs it.
- **F-A8 — Attitude precedence.** For attitude questions the Baseline check precedes the grade check: pre-ratification an attitude finding is `null + BASELINE_UNRATIFIED_SOURCE` even when the means' cell is also 0 (the design's §4.2 posture: the fabric reports no public pulse it cannot cite). One-line change if Vera prefers the grade reason to win.
- **F-A9 — Freshness 0 = unreachable.** A means grading freshness 0 rejects at commission with `NO_RETURN_PATH` (reading of §5.6's "unreachable") rather than commissioning a delivery that can only null.
- **F-A10 — Insider payment edge.** The offer pays the person's business `companyCashRubars` at delivery; if the treasury/payee records are ABSENT from the delivery snapshot the builder throws (assembly breach — fail loud, never skip a payment). A payee corporation dissolved mid-window still receives the credit on its record (the record stands; distribution is the corporation layer's problem) — flagged, not resolved.
- **F-A11 — R-29 grant path unbuilt.** §8.5's grant methodRef (`grant:<grantId>@<revision>`) has no Phase 4 substrate: commission schemas carry no grantRef, so a grant-based collection cannot be commissioned in Phase 4 at all — named absence, consistent with P4-K8. The R-29 handler is a one-function deferral statement, not a rule handler in substance; the descriptor is the stub.
- **F-A12 — NPC commissions refuse by name.** `MANDATE_DATA_ABSENT` (design §3.6/C-9) on all three commissioning kinds; the owner-actor path is complete.
- **F-A13 — Grading prose vs. coded rows.** §5.2's axis tables are prose with more rows than records can decide (e.g., reputation nuances). The coded modulations are exactly: residency, live-connection presence, counter-means × law ≥ 75 (Access 0), no-relationship (Access ≤ 1), freshness return-path classes, and the §5.3 matrix. Everything else rides the establishment profile's stored grades, fixed at creation and immutable in effect (a profile is never re-graded). Every basis row that fires is recorded verbatim in the frozen grading — preview, Log, and intel record show the same rows (P4-G8-C2).
- **F-A14 — Gate 1's decidable rows.** The vehicle-vanish plausibility row is NOT decidable at commission under the one-pass seed contract (vehicle records are not in the commission snapshot), so gate 1's coded rows are the scope-reach rows. Vehicle paths ride the delivery spec's `readPaths`, so a mid-window vanish is visible at delivery.
